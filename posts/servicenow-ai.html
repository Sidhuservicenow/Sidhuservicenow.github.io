What is ServiceNow AI? A Practical Introduction

September 2026 · 6 min read

ServiceNow AI is an umbrella for capabilities that use machine learning, natural language, generative AI and automation to help people work with the Now Platform.

Why developers should care

AI becomes especially useful when it is connected to enterprise workflows, data, permissions and actions. ServiceNow provides a platform where conversational experiences, workflow automation and enterprise records can be brought together.

Key areas to understand

  • Now Assist: Generative AI experiences embedded across supported ServiceNow workflows.
  • AI Agents: Agentic experiences designed to reason across tasks and take actions within defined boundaries.
  • Virtual Agent: Conversational experiences for helping users complete tasks and find information.
  • AI Search: Search and discovery capabilities across ServiceNow content.
  • Document Intelligence: Capabilities for extracting information from documents and using that information in workflows.
