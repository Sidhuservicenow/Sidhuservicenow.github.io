About the author

Hi, I'm Sidhartha Ananthula.

I'm a ServiceNow developer interested in how platform capabilities, thoughtful engineering, and AI can come together to improve enterprise workflows.

This website is where I share technical explorations, implementation approaches, architecture ideas, and developer-focused guides. The goal is to make complex topics easier to understand and give other developers a useful starting point for their own work.

Topics evolve as the platform and AI ecosystem evolve. Articles are intended as practical learning resources; always validate implementation details against your ServiceNow release, instance configuration, and organizational security requirements.
